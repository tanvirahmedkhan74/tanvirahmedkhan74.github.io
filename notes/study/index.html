Study notes

Working through mathematics, rendering and representation learning by writing the explanation I wanted to read.

These notes are how I learn a topic properly: a concrete example first, then the notation, then the worked arithmetic, then the parts I still find hard. Every figure is generated from the geometry or data it describes, and every claim about a method is cited to the paper that made it. They are study notes, separate from my own research.

How these notes are written

Each note opens with a concrete example in plain words, introduces notation only once the idea is in place, works at least one example through by hand, and ends with the references it relies on. Diagrams and animations are produced from code so the picture and the equation cannot drift apart. Where a note shows a synthetic point cloud or a toy matrix, the caption says so; none of them are training runs or benchmark results.

Terms are defined before they are used, equations carry a plain-words reading for screen readers, and every animation has a static poster and a reduced-motion still.
