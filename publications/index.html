CiteCheck — Turning protein function predictions into testable residue-level hypotheses

Co-authored manuscript in preparation; lead author Mahir Shahriar Tamim, Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering, North South University.

A protein-to-text model can say what a protein does, but the statement cannot be tested at a bench unless it names the residues it rests on and actually depends on them. CiteCheck adds a grounding layer that decides whether to make a site claim, and how to word it, from a re-encoding of the cited residues alone, so that the claim becomes a residue-level hypothesis. The evaluation hides each model's own cited residues and re-runs the pipeline, on a clan-held-out test set and on sites curated after the training snapshot.
