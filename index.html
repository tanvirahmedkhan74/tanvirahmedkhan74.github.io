I study how representation, evidence access, and deployment constraints affect what multimodal systems can reliably learn or retrieve. My current work examines evidence-aware retrieval and computational memory; earlier work spans multimodal optimization, low-resource NLP, speech-driven avatars, and efficient inference.

Applying for Fall 2027 Ph.D. positions; thesis-based M.Sc. opportunities also considered.

Research

Current focus

Machine memory, retrieval, and evidence-aware reasoning

Current independent work on interventions that separate routing utility from direct evidence utility, with prospectively gated evaluation on multi-hop question answering.

Multimodal learning

Optimization and evaluation under imbalanced or unreliable modalities, supported by work on calibrated fusion and context-aware Bangla crisis sentiment analysis.

Speech-driven visual systems

Applied research on representation bridges, streaming semantics, fixed-identity rendering, and constrained deployment for conversational avatars.
