Speech2Avatar — Speech-Driven 3D Talking-Head Generation

Machine Intelligence Lab · Ongoing research · Mar 2026 – present

Ongoing Research

How should speech be mapped into facial geometry so that expression and identity survive the rendering step — from parameterized expressions into Gaussian-splatted appearance?

Research direction. Working along the audio → facial-representation → rendering chain: person-specific audio-to-expression models, FLAME/3DMM and Pixel3DMM-based facial tracking, and Gaussian-Splatting-based talking-head rendering. The open question is where expression information is best represented — in a parametric face, in a learned latent, or in the splat field itself.

Systems component. The same line carries a deployment track on NVIDIA Jetson AGX Orin (64 GB, JetPack 6.2): Jetson-native installation of the speech and avatar stack, a latency-attribution benchmark for 2-second chunks, and comparison of a teacher renderer against distilled fixed-identity student variants. Measured renderer-level speedups of roughly 20–28× for student variants; the end-to-end pipeline remained short of real-time, with the remaining bottleneck in the upstream batch-1 speech decode rather than the renderer. Quantization experiments (4-bit / Triton-INT4 kernels) and a benchmarked power-mode correction fed this diagnosis; TensorRT export is the documented next step.

  • Methods3D Gaussian Splatting · FLAME · Pixel3DMM · PyTorch · CUDA · Jetson AGX Orin
  • EstablishedPerson-specific tracking and animation pipelines; multi-GPU inference harnesses; Jetson benchmarks and bottleneck attribution
  • Still openReal-time end-to-end inference; final engine qualification; external evaluation; a paper is not yet claimed

PNEUMA — Perceptual Noesis for Episodic Universal Memory Architecture

Undergraduate thesis · North South University · defended Dec 2025 · supervised by Dr. Nabeel Mohammed

Thesis

Can an agent ask "where did I leave my keys?" and get a temporally grounded answer from years of egocentric video — on consumer hardware?

PNEUMA is a model-agnostic long-term video memory system: video is segmented into chunks with attention-based frame selection, then stored in complementary tiers — structured temporal/action attributes (SQL), week-indexed semantic search (FAISS), and temporal-sequence relations (knowledge graphs). An adaptive cost-based query planner chooses the cheapest reliable retrieval path per query, so language questions over long archives remain retrievable without a cloud dependency.

  • MethodsSQL · FAISS · Knowledge Graphs · Vision-Language Models · PyTorch
  • StatusCompleted capstone thesis — manuscript unpublished; larger-scale evaluations remain future work

Predictive Representation Learning for Long-Video Understanding

Ongoing research · 2026 – present

Ongoing

Video understanding models reason over short contexts: long-horizon understanding, retrieval, and grounding need representations that predict what matters next rather than reconstruct what is already there.

Investigating JEPA-style predictive objectives for video representations, combined with hierarchical memory and structured retrieval, aimed at efficient long-horizon video reasoning and queryability. As with PNEUMA, this folds into the same ambition: a memory-driven intelligence that stays tractable on modest hardware.

  • MethodsJEPA / Predictive Representations · Hierarchical Memory · Retrieval
  • StatusOngoing research — exploratory phase, no results claimed

HiberMem — Interaction Structure in Agent Memory

Ongoing research · 2026 – present

Ongoing

Do higher-order interaction structures among external memories carry predictive or causal information about future agent behavior beyond the individual importance of each memory?

Inspired by hibernation-style retention in biological memory, the project narrows a broad memory-retention concept to a falsifiable question: whether memory interactions can be recovered and measured, and whether they matter for held-out tasks. Controlled synthetic experiments and small-model probes are run first; the full graph/hypergraph memory system is deliberately gated behind those results.

  • MethodsInteraction/attribution metrics · Synthetic task probes · LLM-based memory agents
  • StatusOngoing research — early gated experiments; retention claims deliberately unasserted until qualified

Real-Time Talking Avatars for Robotic Faces

Industry research · RobotBulls × NSU Machine Intelligence Lab · 2025 – 2026

Industry Research

Interactive streaming avatars need lip sync across many audio chunks, aligned to a reference video — yet the standard inference path assumes a single GPU and a single pass over the whole input.

The program reworked a MuseTalk-based pipeline so that processing no longer assumes one GPU: audio processing on one instance, generation spread over multiple GPU instances (tested up to five), and final frames assembled from the distributed outputs. The setup exposes a streaming HTTP interface and was reported able to keep end-to-end responses under 4 seconds, with silence handling and interruption behavior implemented. Distributed testing was a particular difficulty — output quality became dependent on external network characteristics that the pipeline could not control.

  • My involvementMulti-GPU inference pipeline development and chunked execution for generative workloads (per CV); work in a lab team across the program
  • StatusOperational pipeline; silence integration and distributed-environment robustness remained open

Audio2Face-Driven 3D Avatar & Blendshape Recovery

Industry research · RobotBulls × NSU Machine Intelligence Lab · 2025 – 2026

Industry Research

A real-time, cloud-deployable 3D avatar with natural facial motion — and converting the output of an audio-driven model into a form animators can actually use.

The team integrated an Audio2Face-trained model with a Three.js visualization pipeline, and decoded vertices to ARKit-style blendshape weights via linear optimization — reported at ~90% agreement with the microservice output on the tested model. Practical issues dominated: 3D model format/texturing (glb/usd), and measurable latency between audio and rendered animation.

  • My involvementAudio2Face × Three.js integration and blendshape-recovery investigation (per CV)
  • StatusFunctioning prototype; blendshape computation was heavy for long sequences and required per-model tuning

Geometry & Evaluation for Silent Segments

Industry research · RobotBulls × NSU Machine Intelligence Lab · 2025 – 2026

Industry Research

Lip-sync models keep talking during silence. There is no standard geometric metric for how natural idle motion stays when there is no audio to anchor it.

A geometric, spatio-temporal metric (v1) was built for silent audio segments and improved after its first version exposed inconsistencies; comprehensive benchmarking against other lip-sync and talking-face models was still pending at the time of the collaboration record.

  • StatusExperimental — metric designed and hardened; broad benchmarking remained unfinished

LatentSync / MuseTalk Fine-Tuning Streams

Industry research · RobotBulls × NSU Machine Intelligence Lab · 2025 – 2026

Industry Research

Stock open lip-sync models plateau quickly at the mouth region with real-time constraint — improving mouth fidelity without losing speed was the target.

LatentSync was re-trained at 512×512 with a custom dataset, MuseTalk at 512×512, and custom UNet/diffusion-transformer mouth refiners were explored. Gains were marginal against costs: small, unbalanced datasets, expensive H200 training, and unresolved temporal consistency.

  • StatusExploratory — paused/abandoned per the collaboration record; the honest takeaway was that lip sync, identity, latency, and temporal stability are separate unsolved objectives
