Speech2Avatar — Speech-Driven 3D Talking-Head Generation

Machine Intelligence Lab · Ongoing research · Mar 2026 – present

Ongoing Research

How should speech be mapped into facial geometry so that expression and identity survive the rendering step — from parameterized expressions into Gaussian-splatted appearance?

Research direction. Working along the audio → facial-representation → rendering chain: person-specific audio-to-expression models, FLAME/3DMM and Pixel3DMM-based facial tracking, and Gaussian-Splatting-based talking-head rendering. The open question is where expression information is best represented — in a parametric face, in a learned latent, or in the splat field itself.

Systems component. A deployment track on NVIDIA Jetson AGX Orin (64 GB, JetPack 6.2): latency attribution for 2-second chunks and comparison of a teacher renderer against distilled fixed-identity students. Student variants reached roughly 20–28× renderer-level speedups; the end-to-end pipeline remained short of real-time, with the bottleneck in upstream batch-1 speech decoding rather than rendering. 4-bit/Triton-INT4 quantization and a power-mode correction fed this diagnosis; TensorRT export is the documented next step.

  • Methods3D Gaussian Splatting · FLAME · Pixel3DMM · PyTorch · CUDA · Jetson AGX Orin
  • EstablishedPerson-specific tracking and animation pipelines; multi-GPU inference harnesses; Jetson benchmarks and bottleneck attribution
  • Still openReal-time end-to-end inference; external evaluation; a paper is not yet claimed

PNEUMA — Perceptual Noesis for Episodic Universal Memory Architecture

Undergraduate thesis · North South University · defended Dec 2025 · supervised by Dr. Nabeel Mohammed

Thesis

Can an agent ask "where did I leave my keys?" and get a temporally grounded answer from years of egocentric video — on consumer hardware?

PNEUMA is a model-agnostic long-term video memory system. Video is segmented into chunks with attention-based frame selection; each chunk is described by aspect-specific embeddings (action, object, spatial, audio, visual) and stored across three complementary tiers — structured temporal and action attributes (SQL), week-indexed semantic search (FAISS), and temporal-sequence relations (knowledge graphs). An adaptive cost-based query planner selects a retrieval path per query, so natural-language questions over long archives stay answerable on consumer hardware, without a cloud dependency.

  • MethodsSQL · FAISS · Knowledge Graphs · Vision-Language Models · PyTorch
  • StatusCompleted capstone thesis — manuscript unpublished; larger-scale evaluations remain future work

About

Research trajectory

I came into research through competitive programming — taking systems apart and reasoning about their components — and then through coursework in deep learning and pattern recognition, where knowledge distillation for object localization became my first research prototype.

At the Machine Intelligence Lab I moved from reading papers to building under real constraints: speech-driven talking heads, distributed GPU inference, and the latency and memory limits of edge hardware. Building PNEUMA for long egocentric video turned a practical question into a research one: what should a computational memory retain, and how should it structure what it keeps?

That question — perception and memory under realistic compute budgets — is what I want to pursue in graduate research: multimodal learning that stays balanced under unreliable supervision, video understanding over long horizons, and generation that runs in real time. My goal is a career in academic research.
