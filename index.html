Tanvir Ahmed Khan

Representation learning, evidence use and speech-driven visual interaction.

I study how learned representations preserve useful information, how downstream systems use it, and how these choices affect retrieval and speech-driven visual interaction. My independent research develops alongside applied conversational-avatar work at the Machine Intelligence Lab (MILab), North South University.

I am a Research Assistant at MILab, North South University, advised by Dr. Nabeel Mohammed and Dr. Shafin Rahman. I completed my B.Sc. in Computer Science and Engineering in December 2025 (CGPA 3.80/4.00, Summa Cum Laude).

For 2027 I am looking for a group working on multimodal learning, memory and retrieval, long-video understanding, or neural rendering.

What does a reranker encode that its scoring head does not use?

Applied research and background

Since March 2025, my work at MILab has combined model integration, controlled investigations and real-time system development. These applied problems also inform my independent research on evidence use, renderer measurements and information available at playback deadlines.

Our work spans high-resolution talking heads, streaming speech-to-face bridges, Gaussian and FLAME investigations, renderer distillation, embedded optimization, and retrieval/tool integration. I took primary technical responsibility for substantial early work; the final Orin customization and RAG integration reflect major team contributions.

Explore MILab applied projects →

Before and alongside this research, I worked remotely as a Junior Software Engineer at Tuctuc (January 2022–June 2026), developing Odoo ERP and React Native applications. My completed undergraduate thesis, PNEUMA, studied searchable memory for long video in a four-person team.
