Tanvir Ahmed Khan

Research Assistant, Machine Intelligence Lab
North South University · Dhaka, Bangladesh

I work on multimodal learning, retrieval, and speech-driven visual systems. At MILab, I am advised by Dr. Nabeel Mohammed and Dr. Shafin Rahman. My work includes multimodal fusion, low-resource language understanding, and the engineering of conversational avatars.

My current research examines evidence loss in multi-hop question answering (ERL), the relationship between speech and facial motion (RIFT), and capacity growth in verified language-model self-improvement (RSI).

Research interests. Multimodal and representation learning; memory and retrieval; long-video understanding; 3D vision and neural rendering. I am also interested in predictive representations and world models, as exploratory directions.

I am applying for Fall 2027 Ph.D. positions and also considering thesis-based M.Sc. opportunities.

Selected research

ERL — Evidence Loss in Multi-Hop QA

Ongoing study of where multi-hop question answering loses useful evidence. Further experiments examine whether selection improvements survive stronger baselines and improve final answers.

Research ongoing · Manuscript in preparation

RIFT — Recoverability-Gated Implicit Facial Dynamics

An exploratory measurement study of speech-predictable facial motion and its effect on rendered articulation. Following a negative predictive pilot, the current RIFT-M focus is measurement reliability.

Active exploratory research · RIFT-M measurement study

RSI — Capacity Growth in Verified Self-Improvement

Investigating whether function-preserving model expansion can restore learning during verified self-training. Current work establishes reliable training data and experiment recovery before testing the growth hypothesis.

Engineering pilot · Growth hypothesis untested

Speech-Driven Avatar Systems

A MILab and industry research-engineering trajectory spanning representation bridges, streaming, interruption semantics, renderer distillation, and Jetson profiling.

Applied research & systems · Portions closed source

All research and projects, including PNEUMA, AirFLOW, DynApex, and the StreamTalker-GS negative-result study.
