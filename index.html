Research

Research interests

Machine memory, retrieval, and evidence-aware reasoning

Current independent work on interventions that separate routing utility from direct evidence utility, with prospectively gated evaluation on multi-hop question answering.

Multimodal learning and low-resource language understanding

Optimization and evaluation under imbalanced or unreliable modalities, supported by work on calibrated fusion and context-aware Bangla crisis sentiment analysis.

Predictive representation learning and long-video understanding

Research interest in compact temporal representations and memory over long horizons, kept distinct from the completed and discontinued PNEUMA thesis direction.

Speech-driven visual systems, 3D vision, and efficient inference

Ongoing RIFT research on recoverable motion and residual uncertainty, alongside applied work on representation bridges, neural rendering, and efficient conversational avatars.
