\section{Profile}
{\RaggedRight\hyphenpenalty=10000\exhyphenpenalty=10000\relax
I am a Research Assistant at the Machine Intelligence Lab, where I build and measure
speech-driven talking-avatar systems. My own research asks one question in several
settings: when a model retains useful information in an intermediate representation, can the next
stage actually use it? I study this in multi-hop question answering, where supporting evidence can
be retrieved and still never reach the answer, and in talking-head renderers, where a hard feature
selection changes what a measurement means. I have \textbf{four co-authored papers} in
\textit{Neurocomputing}, \textit{PLOS ONE}, AACL-IJCNLP 2026 and IEEE ICCIT 2025, two first-author
manuscripts in preparation and two further first-author studies in progress. I am seeking a
\textbf{\cvSeeking}.
\par}

\wip{A Frozen-Index Audit of Finite-Difference Probes in One Released Talking-Head Renderer}
{Lead author\enspace\textperiodcentered\enspace Manuscript in preparation}
{A finite-difference probe through this renderer can cross a hard feature selection. Holding the

{Lead author\enspace\textperiodcentered\enspace Work in progress}

far less than the margin set in advance.}

\section{Undergraduate Thesis}
\entryHeading{\href{https://tanvirahmedkhan74.github.io/research/pneuma/}{PNEUMA --- Video memory
for long-form question answering}}{Completed Dec 2025}
\par\nobreak\vspace{1pt}
{\small\RaggedRight\hyphenpenalty=10000\exhyphenpenalty=10000\relax
Four-person thesis supervised by Dr.~Nabeel Mohammed. We stored pretrained-model descriptions of a
video in SQL records, vector indices and a temporal graph, so that one moment could be retrieved by
action, object, place, audio or time. Our evaluation left grounding and consistency unresolved; that
question now drives my work on evidence selection.\par}

{Wrote streaming adapters between speech models (Moshi, PersonaPlex) and renderers (MuseTalk,
IMTalker, Gaussian avatars) and distilled the IMTalker renderer. Once the renderer was faster, the
dominant cost moved upstream to speech generation.}
