ERL — Emergent Retrieval Landmark

Studying graph routing, evidence selection, and reader sensitivity for multi-hop question answering on LoCoMo, MuSiQue, and 2WikiMultihopQA. Initial routing matched BM25 on the held-out LoCoMo test; fixed-pool selection gains depend on the dataset and reader.

RIFT — Recoverability-Gated Implicit Facial Dynamics

Investigating recoverable motion and residual uncertainty in IMTalker's implicit motion space for audio-driven talking heads. A completed 36-fit temporal pilot did not support the tested hypothesis; representation-fidelity and residual-dynamics studies are planned before generative modeling.
