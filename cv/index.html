Profile

I am a Research Assistant at the Machine Intelligence Lab, North South University, where I build and measure speech-driven talking-avatar systems. My own research asks one question in two settings: when a model retains useful information in an intermediate representation, can the next stage actually use it? I study this in multi-hop question answering, where supporting evidence can be retrieved and still never reach the answer, and in talking avatars, where the speech model’s states and the renderer’s measurements decide what the face can do. I have four co-authored papers in Neurocomputing, PLOS ONE, AACL-IJCNLP 2026 and IEEE ICCIT 2025, and first-author manuscripts in preparation on both questions. I am seeking a Ph.D. or research-based M.Sc. position for 2027.

Vision and language: computer vision and natural language processing; image and video representation learning; multimodal fusion; long-video understanding; evidence selection and memory for question answering.

Responsible AI: faithfulness and evaluation; measurement reliability; grounding statements in testable evidence.

Applications: speech-driven visual interaction; AI for healthcare and life sciences.

Research in progress

Evidence use in multi-hop question answering

From Transit Routing to Evidence Selection: Conditional Audits of Multi-Hop QA Failures

Lead author · Manuscript in preparation

Separate audits of evidence access, selection and reading. Learned graph routing tied BM25 at a matched budget; a selection add-on gained little over fine-tuned cross-encoders, and no end-to-end answer gain was established.

Follow-up: Capacity-Gated Sparse Weight Repair for Multi-Hop Evidence Reranking

Lead author · Preregistered

A linear probe on a frozen reranker recovers evidence signal that its scoring head does not use; confirmation pending.

Speech-driven talking avatars

Deadline-Available Speech States for Full-Duplex Avatar Articulation

Lead author · Manuscript in preparation

Most of the apparent lookahead a streaming speech model offers for lip motion comes from its decoding schedule and costs no delay; waiting a further 160 ms improved lip-aperture prediction by far less than the margin set in advance.

A Frozen-Index Audit of Finite-Difference Probes in One Released Talking-Head Renderer

Lead author · Manuscript in preparation

A finite-difference probe through this renderer can cross a hard feature selection. Holding the selection fixed removed most of the measured response at the first set of states; the replication was partial, and the original local-linearity check still fails.

Grounding predictions in testable evidence

Turning Protein Function Predictions into Testable Residue-Level Hypotheses

Co-author · Manuscript in preparation · Lead author: Mahir Shahriar Tamim

Ties a predicted protein-function statement to the residues that support it and tests whether the statement depends on them.

Undergraduate thesis

PNEUMA — Video memory for long-form question answering
Completed December 2025

Four-person thesis supervised by Dr. Nabeel Mohammed; I designed the methodology and pipeline and led the work. We stored pretrained-model descriptions of a video in SQL records, vector indices and a temporal graph. Our evaluation left grounding and consistency unresolved; that question now drives my work on evidence selection.

Applied research experience

Research Assistant · Machine Intelligence Lab, North South University
March 2025–present
Working on conversational avatars. Advisors: Dr. Nabeel Mohammed and Dr. Shafin Rahman.

Conversational pipelines and response delayPrimary; robot head with the team

Built the streaming talking-avatar serving path on MuseTalk and LatentSync and cut its estimated end-to-end response delay from about 30 s to about 4 s on a multi-GPU server. The avatar face then ran on an industry partner’s robot head.

  • MuseTalk 512×512 training and high-resolution development
  • MuseTalk/LatentSync conversational pipeline and multi-GPU serving
  • Streaming avatar on a humanoid robot face

Audio-driven 3D avatars and blendshape controlPrimary responsibility

Drove 3D character rigs from speech through NVIDIA’s Audio2Face and recovered 52 blendshape weights per frame as a regularised inverse problem. The recovered weights came out damped against the reference.

  • Audio2Face-driven 3D avatars in the browser
  • Recovering blendshape controls from vertex animation

Face representation and motion stabilityPrimary responsibility

Ran controlled training and rendering variants on speech-driven Gaussian avatars, with Pixel3DMM and FLAME fitting, to separate fitting, motion representation and rendering as sources of unwanted head motion.

  • Head-wobble stabilization investigations
  • Pixel3DMM fitting and stabilization
  • Custom FLAME articulation

Speech-to-motion bridges and renderer costPrimary responsibility

Wrote streaming adapters between speech models (Moshi, PersonaPlex) and renderers (MuseTalk, IMTalker, Gaussian avatars) and distilled the IMTalker renderer. Once the renderer was faster, the dominant cost moved upstream to speech generation.

  • Gaussian avatar–Moshi integration
  • Moshi–MuseTalk, Moshi–IMTalker and PersonaPlex–IMTalker bridges
  • IMTalker renderer distillation

Embedded real-time execution on Jetson AGX OrinPrimary, then team

Wrote the export tooling and TensorRT optimization; the team then delivered the real-time PersonaPlex–IMTalker system.

  • Jetson AGX Orin export and TensorRT optimization
  • Real-time PersonaPlex–IMTalker customization on Orin
