Applied research experience — MILab

Research Assistant · Machine Intelligence Lab, North South University
March 2025–present
Advisors: Dr. Nabeel Mohammed and Dr. Shafin Rahman.

Our work spans conversational avatars, 3D representation and embedded inference; responsibility for each strand is given beside its heading.

Conversational pipelines and response delayPrimary responsibility

Building and serving the streaming talking-avatar pipeline, through to multi-GPU serving and an industry partner’s robot head.

  • MuseTalk/LatentSync conversational pipeline and multi-GPU serving

Audio-driven 3D avatars and blendshape controlPrimary responsibility

Driving 3D character rigs from speech through NVIDIA’s Audio2Face, and recovering named blendshape controls from its output.

  • Audio2Face-driven 3D avatars in the browser
  • Recovering blendshape controls from vertex animation

Face representation and motion stabilityPrimary responsibility

Fitting and representation work on the avatar face, with controlled investigations into unwanted head motion; failures retained.

  • Head-wobble stabilization investigations
  • Pixel3DMM fitting and stabilization
  • Custom FLAME articulation

Speech-to-motion bridges and renderer costPrimary responsibility

Streaming adapters between speech models and renderers, and a distillation study isolating what the renderer itself costs.

  • Gaussian avatar–Moshi integration
  • Moshi–MuseTalk, Moshi–IMTalker and PersonaPlex–IMTalker bridges
  • IMTalker renderer distillation

Embedded real-time execution on Jetson AGX OrinPrimary, then team

Export tooling and TensorRT optimization on embedded hardware, then the delivered real-time system.

  • Jetson AGX Orin export and TensorRT optimization
  • Real-time PersonaPlex–IMTalker customization on Orin

Retrieval and tool use in the conversationTeam effort
