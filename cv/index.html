First-author manuscripts in preparation

From Transit Routing to Evidence Selection: Conditional Audits of Multi-Hop QA Failures

First-author manuscript in preparation · Planned target: ACL

I trace evidence loss across candidate access, selection and reading in multi-hop question answering. Controlled comparisons distinguish improvements in evidence selection from the conditions under which a reader can use that evidence.

I lead the research design, implementation and analysis. The manuscript preserves negative routing findings and qualifies selection gains against stronger baselines and reader controls.

Deadline-Available Speech States for Full-Duplex Avatar Articulation

First-author work in progress · Alternative planned targets: NeurIPS 2027 or ICCV 2027

I examine articulation-relevant information in streaming speech-model states at a playback deadline and after additional waiting. The broader programme studies how this information can be used under explicit timing constraints.

I lead the research design and implementation. A completed pilot diagnostic is distinct from the expanded programme now in development.

Independent research in progress

Capacity-Gated Sparse Weight Repair for Multi-Hop Evidence Reranking

Active independent research

I investigate the gap between support-selection information encoded in a frozen reranker and the information used by its scoring head. The experiments compare supervised readouts with constrained persistent weight edits.

I design and implement the controlled comparisons, evaluation infrastructure and constrained-editing experiments.

Finite-difference probes across hard feature selection: a frozen-index audit of one released talking-head renderer

Lead research · Manuscript in preparation · Planned target: TMLR

I study the reliability of finite-difference measurements in a released talking-head renderer. Holding feature selections fixed helps separate changes in discrete choices from responses along continuous pathways.

I lead the experimental design, implementation and interpretation, retaining negative and ambiguous findings.

Applied research experience — MILab

Research Assistant · Machine Intelligence Lab, North South University
March 2025–present
Advisors: Dr. Nabeel Mohammed and Dr. Shafin Rahman.

Our work spans conversational avatars, 3D representation and embedded inference. I held primary technical responsibility in earlier integration and investigation work, and contributed within the team to the later real-time Orin customization and RAG/tool integration. These systems investigations inform my independent research on representation, measurement and timing.

  • MuseTalk 512×512 training and high-resolution development — High-resolution talking-head development using the MuseTalk foundation.
  • MuseTalk/LatentSync conversational pipeline — Conversational speech and video queues, TTS integration and server-side response-delay engineering, followed by a multi-GPU system.
  • Gaussian avatar–Moshi integration — Connecting conversational speech generation to a GaussianTalker-based avatar through Moshi/Mimi components.
  • Head-wobble stabilization investigations — Controlled investigations of unwanted head motion in speech-driven Gaussian avatars.
  • Pixel3DMM fitting and stabilization — Using Pixel3DMM as an upstream fitting foundation to investigate face representation and motion stability.
  • Custom FLAME articulation — Custom articulation integration and controlled investigations using the FLAME face model.
  • Moshi–MuseTalk integration — A streaming bridge between conversational speech-model output and MuseTalk-based face generation.
  • Moshi–IMTalker integration — A separate speech-feature and motion bridge from Moshi to IMTalker.
  • PersonaPlex–IMTalker integration — Connecting PersonaPlex conversational output with the IMTalker motion and rendering pipeline.
  • IMTalker renderer distillation — Fixed-identity teacher–student renderer development and component-level benchmark design.
  • Jetson AGX Orin export and TensorRT optimization — Export, engine integration, precision/graph checks and profiling for constrained embedded execution.
  • PersonaPlex–IMTalker real-time Orin customization — Our collaborative customization of PersonaPlex–IMTalker for a real-time Jetson AGX Orin system.
  • RAG and tool calling for conversational avatars — Our collaborative work connecting retrieval, external tools and conditioning to the avatar conversation pipeline.
