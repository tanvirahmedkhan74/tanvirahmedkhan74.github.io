MuseTalk 512×512 training and high-resolution development

High-resolution talking-head development using the MuseTalk foundation.

In our early MILab work, I took primary technical responsibility for the 512×512 training and high-resolution development workstream.

MuseTalk/LatentSync conversational pipeline

Conversational speech and video queues, TTS integration and server-side response-delay engineering, followed by a multi-GPU system.

I had primary technical responsibility for substantial parts of our earlier pipeline integration and latency work.

In our earlier server development, I estimated response delay at approximately 30 seconds initially and 4 seconds in the later multi-GPU system. These are historical development estimates; the Orin work is a separate system.

Workstream contents ↑

Gaussian avatar–Moshi integration

Connecting conversational speech generation to a GaussianTalker-based avatar through Moshi/Mimi components.

I led substantial integration and streaming-development work within our MILab collaboration.

Workstream contents ↑

Head-wobble stabilization investigations

Controlled investigations of unwanted head motion in speech-driven Gaussian avatars.

I developed and examined stabilization variants, including failure cases, to understand unwanted head motion.

Workstream contents ↑

Pixel3DMM fitting and stabilization

Using Pixel3DMM as an upstream fitting foundation to investigate face representation and motion stability.

I worked on the fitting and representation pathway and its integration into our avatar investigations.

Workstream contents ↑

Custom FLAME articulation

Custom articulation integration and controlled investigations using the FLAME face model.

I took primary technical responsibility for this early articulation work, examining how motion representations interact with the avatar pipeline.

Workstream contents ↑

Moshi–MuseTalk integration

A streaming bridge between conversational speech-model output and MuseTalk-based face generation.

I developed streaming execution and adapter infrastructure, with attention to chunk boundaries and buffering.

Workstream contents ↑

Moshi–IMTalker integration

A separate speech-feature and motion bridge from Moshi to IMTalker.

I worked on feature adaptation, chunk contracts and streaming integration in our earlier IMTalker pipeline.

Workstream contents ↑

PersonaPlex–IMTalker integration

Connecting PersonaPlex conversational output with the IMTalker motion and rendering pipeline.

I carried substantial technical responsibility for this integration stage, before the later collaborative Orin customization.

Workstream contents ↑

IMTalker renderer distillation

Fixed-identity teacher–student renderer development and component-level benchmark design.

I worked on renderer distillation and on separating renderer cost from broader conversational response timing.

Workstream contents ↑

Jetson AGX Orin export and TensorRT optimization

Export, engine integration, precision/graph checks and profiling for constrained embedded execution.

I developed and examined deployment and optimization paths, treating hardware configuration and the measured timing stage explicitly.

Workstream contents ↑

PersonaPlex–IMTalker real-time Orin customization

Our collaborative customization of PersonaPlex–IMTalker for a real-time Jetson AGX Orin system.

I contributed to our team’s integration and system-development work. The final system reflects major team contributions.

Workstream contents ↑

RAG and tool calling for conversational avatars

Our collaborative work connecting retrieval, external tools and conditioning to the avatar conversation pipeline.

I participated in this team workstream; retrieval, tool access and live speech scheduling were developed collaboratively.

Foundations and attribution

This work builds on MuseTalk, LatentSync, GaussianTalker, Moshi/Mimi, Pixel3DMM, FLAME, IMTalker and PersonaPlex. My contribution is in the integration, investigations and systems work described above; the underlying models retain their original attribution.
