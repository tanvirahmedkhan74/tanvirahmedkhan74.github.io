Deadline articulation — Information available at playback

What can an avatar know when speech becomes playable, and what does waiting add?

First-author work in progress · Alternative planned targets: NeurIPS 2027 or ICCV 2027

On this page

  • Research question
  • Study & contribution
  • Manuscript & scope

Research question

I examine articulation-relevant information in streaming speech-model states at a playback deadline and after additional waiting. The broader programme studies how this information can be used under explicit timing constraints.

Study and contribution

I lead the research design and implementation. A completed pilot diagnostic is distinct from the expanded programme now in development.

The pilot diagnostic asks what changes under matched information. The expanded programme develops trained-head and scheduling evaluations under playback constraints. These are separate stages of the same developing study.
