Research in progress

First-author manuscript in preparation

ERL — Evidence selection in multi-hop question answering

Where does supporting evidence disappear before a question is answered?

Learned graph routing tied BM25 at a matched budget. A selection add-on that helped zero-shot scorers gained little over fine-tuned cross-encoders, and no end-to-end answer gain was established.

First-author study in progress · Preregistered, two-hop scope

CG-SWR — Representation and readout gaps in evidence reranking

What does a reranker encode that its scoring head does not use?

A linear probe on a frozen reranker recovers evidence-selection signal that its scoring head does not use. Trained heads recover most of that gap and random edits none. The sealed confirmation is pending.

First-author manuscript in preparation

Deadline articulation — Information available at playback

What can an avatar know when speech becomes playable, and what does waiting add?

Most of the apparent lookahead a streaming speech model offers for lip motion comes from its decoding schedule and costs no delay. Waiting a further 160 ms improved lip-aperture prediction by about a quarter of the margin set in advance.

First-author manuscript in preparation

RIFT — Reliability of renderer measurements

When does a small input change cross a hidden discrete choice?

Holding the renderer’s hard feature selections fixed removed most of the measured finite-difference response at the first set of states. The replication was partial, and the original local-linearity check still fails.

A co-authored manuscript in preparation, CiteCheck, ties protein-function predictions to the residues that support them; its lead author is Mahir Shahriar Tamim.

Undergraduate thesis

PNEUMA — Video memory for long-form question answering. A four-person thesis supervised by Dr. Nabeel Mohammed, completed in December 2025; I designed the methodology and pipeline and led the work. We stored pretrained-model descriptions of a video in SQL records, vector indices and a temporal graph. Our evaluation left grounding and consistency unresolved, and that question became ERL.

Applied research at MILab

At MILab I work on conversational avatars: the serving path and its response delay, audio-driven 3D faces, face representation, the bridges between speech models and renderers, and execution on Jetson AGX Orin. The MILab projects page covers six strands with demo clips. The real-time system on the device and the retrieval and tool work were delivered with the team.
