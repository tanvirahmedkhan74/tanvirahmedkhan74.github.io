Manuscripts and active independent studies

First-author manuscript in preparation · Planned target: ACL

ERL — Evidence selection in multi-hop question answering

Where does supporting evidence disappear before a question is answered?

A conditional audit of where supporting documents are lost on the way to an answer, with selection gains judged against stronger baselines and separate reader conditions.

Active independent research

CG-SWR — Representation and readout gaps in evidence reranking

What does a reranker encode that its scoring head does not use?

Tests whether a frozen reranker's representation keeps support-selection signal that its scalar head discards, comparing trained readouts with small constrained weight edits.

First-author manuscript in preparation · Planned target: TMLR

RIFT — Reliability of renderer measurements

When does a small input change cross a hidden discrete choice?

Asks whether a finite-difference probe through a hard feature selection measures smooth renderer response or a switch in the selected feature, using a frozen-index control in one released renderer.

First-author work in progress · Alternative planned targets: NeurIPS 2027 or ICCV 2027

Deadline articulation — Information available at playback

What can an avatar know when speech becomes playable, and what does waiting add?

A matched-information pilot of what streaming speech states already carry at the playback deadline; trained readouts and timing schedules belong to the planned expanded programme.

Completed undergraduate thesis

PNEUMA — Video memory for long-form question answering. Completed December 2025 with a four-person thesis team under Dr. Nabeel Mohammed. I carried out the core technical implementation; teammates contributed to the real-time JavaScript application.

Applied research at MILab

Our conversational-avatar work connects speech models, face representations, streaming systems and constrained devices. The MILab projects page follows seventeen milestones from serving delay and audio-driven 3D avatars through face representation and speech-to-motion bridges to embedded Orin execution and retrieval. The Orin real-time customization and the retrieval and tool work were delivered by the team.
