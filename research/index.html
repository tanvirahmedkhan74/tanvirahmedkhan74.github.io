Research

Current investigations, collaborative systems work, and earlier studies. Each project page describes the question, contribution, evidence, and remaining limitations.

Ongoing research

ERL — Emergent Retrieval Landmark

Ongoing study of graph routing, evidence selection, and reader sensitivity in multi-hop QA. Initial routing matched BM25 on held-out LoCoMo; fixed-pool selection gains remain dataset- and reader-dependent.

Ongoing research · Independent · 2026–present

RIFT — Recoverability-Gated Implicit Facial Dynamics

Ongoing research on recoverable motion and residual uncertainty in frozen IMTalker's motion space. The completed 36-fit temporal pilot did not support the tested hypothesis; representation fidelity and residual dynamics remain under evaluation.

Ongoing research · 2026–present

Applied research & engineering

Speech-Driven Avatar Systems

A consolidated MILab and industry trajectory: conversational pipelines, representation bridges, multi-GPU streaming, TensorRT export, renderer distillation, Jetson profiling, and a separate 3DGS research branch.

Collaborative applied research · Portions closed source

AirFLOW — Browser-Based Aerodynamic Analysis

Early-stage collaborative engineering developed with an aerospace practitioner and MIST graduate. The workbench turns raw STL geometry and explicit assumptions into a custom horseshoe-VLM analysis and an OpenFOAM case-generation handoff without implying a CFD run.

Early-stage collaboration · Preliminary browser MVP

Earlier and discontinued work

PNEUMA — Video Memory for Long-Form QA

A four-person undergraduate thesis integrating frozen perception models with SQL, vector, and temporal-graph memory. The direction ended after the evaluation audit exposed unresolved correctness and grounding limitations.

Discontinued thesis work · Four-person team

StreamTalker-GS — Speech-Driven 3DGS Experiments

The covariance-transport hypothesis passed footprint checks but failed its residual-association gate across all three tested identities. The improvement claim was frozen and the line archived.

Completed study · Hypothesis not supported
