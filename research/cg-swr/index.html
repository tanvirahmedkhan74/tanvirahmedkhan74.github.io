CG-SWR — Representation and readout gaps in evidence reranking

What does a reranker encode that its scoring head does not use?

First-author study in progress · Preregistered, two-hop scope

On this page

  • What I found
  • The question
  • How the study works
  • My role
  • Manuscript

What I found so far

A small cross-encoder reranker scores a query–passage pair with one scalar head on a pooled state. On two-hop questions, a supervised linear probe on that frozen state ranks supporting passages clearly better than the model’s own head, and it beats shuffled-label, random-encoder and lexical controls. The representation holds evidence-selection signal that the head does not use.

An ordinary trained head recovers most of that gap. A low-rank edit and a new linear head both come close to the probe. Random edits of the same form recover nothing.

These are estimates on the selection split, not confirmed results. The question in the title is still open: whether a constrained edit, found without gradient fitting, adds anything beyond a trained head. The editor analysis and the one-shot sealed confirmation are not finished.

The question

A probe that decodes a label shows that the information is present. It does not show that the model uses it, and a probe can learn the task itself. An edit whose numbers come from a fitted readout is supervised learning written into weights. I wanted a study that could tell these apart.

How the study works

Two interventions are compared under one protocol: a supervised readout trained on the frozen representation, and a constrained edit to a small set of the model’s own parameters. The encoder stays frozen, so any difference between the two comes from how the representation is read and not from new representation learning.

An earlier version of this study did not survive an audit of its own intervention. I rebuilt it as a preregistered protocol with fresh splits, a budgeted selection split and sealed confirmation sets. The scope is narrow on purpose, two-hop retrieval with a single reranker, so that a negative result can still be interpreted.

My role

This is my own project. I designed and implemented the controlled comparisons, the evaluation infrastructure and the constrained-editing experiments.

Manuscript

Working title: Capacity-Gated Sparse Weight Repair for Multi-Hop Evidence Reranking.

The study is in progress. Whether an edit improves downstream question answering is not something it can claim yet. Exact figures stay in the manuscript until it is public.
