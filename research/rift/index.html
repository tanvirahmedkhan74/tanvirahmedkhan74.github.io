RIFT — Recoverability-Gated Implicit Facial Dynamics

Which parts of facial motion can be recovered from audio and motion history, and how should a talking-head model represent the uncertainty that remains?

2026–presentAudio-driven talking headsUpdated 28 September 2026

Current status: ongoing research. The completed 36-fit temporal pilot did not support the tested configuration. The central hypothesis remains unvalidated; the revised experiment plan is prospective.

Separate recoverable motion from residual uncertainty

RIFT investigates motion in the implicit representation of the frozen IMTalker system. The proposal asks whether predictable components can be separated from uncertain residual variation, and whether that separation can guide stochastic motion generation across identities.

The current plan first tests representation fidelity, predictive recoverability, and the way residual errors propagate through rendered motion. Improved generation remains a hypothesis to test after these measurements.

Completed pilot and planned experiments

Completed pilot

A 36-fit temporal study failed its frozen criteria. The summary arithmetic was independently checked, but per-fit trajectories and the full remote report were missing from the locally reviewed record. The cause of failure has not been isolated.

Representation fidelity · planned

Compare source video with oracle renders from frozen motion latents; test measurement repeatability, reference-image robustness, articulation, and identity preservation.

Residual dynamics · planned

Distinguish temporal dependence in prediction residuals, the effect of latent perturbations on rendered motion, and error accumulation when generated history is fed back.

Controlled generation · conditional

After adequate measurement and transferable structure are established, compare allocation-based generation with matched covariance, history-aware, smoothing, and shuffled-allocation controls.

Contributions and open questions

Research work
Hypothesis formulation, temporal-pilot evaluation, failure analysis, and a prospective measurement and residual-dynamics protocol.
External foundation
IMTalker's pretrained encoder, motion representation, and renderer are upstream components.
Still unresolved
Transferable recoverability, useful stochastic allocation, better generation, and stable deployment rollouts have not been demonstrated.
Evidence date
The proposal and 27 September 2026 implementation-and-experiment plan inform this summary. The plan reports no new implementation or experiments executed under it.
