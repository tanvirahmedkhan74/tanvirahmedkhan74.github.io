StreamTalker-GS — Speech-Driven 3DGS Experiments

A controlled study of whether FLAME-driven affine covariance transport created a measurable rendering improvement in person-specific Gaussian avatars.

Completed experimental studyThree tested identities1.04M supported observations

Status: original hypothesis not supported. The method-improvement claim was frozen; this is not presented as a successful new 3DGS method.

Hypothesis

Could affine transport matter beyond similarity transport?

FLAME-bound Gaussian avatars deform local surface geometry as the face moves. The tested hypothesis was that transporting Gaussian covariance with the local affine deformation—rather than a similarity-only approximation—would create a detectable residual association with rendering error and justify a causal renderer swap.

The audit separated four gates: non-similarity of the deformation, a projected footprint large enough to matter, residual association after controlling the tested factors, and a causal swap. Passing an early geometry gate was not treated as proof of downstream image improvement.

Experimental design

Support, association, then intervention

GateQuestionObserved result
H1Does local deformation depart from similarity transport?Supported in the screened identities.
H2Is the projected covariance difference large enough to affect the rasterizer footprint?At least 0.5 px in 31.669%, 37.731%, and 39.493% of supported visible observations.
H3Does the residual geometry signal associate with the tested rendering residual?Rejected in 0 of 3 identities; reported Spearman values −0.8971, +0.3212, and −0.1594 did not satisfy the gate.
H4Does a causal renderer swap improve the outcome?Unexecuted because the preceding gate failed.

Control evidence

Separate analytic-to-rasterizer checks reached a maximum radial RMS difference of 5.02×10−7 px and responded monotonically to filter and overlap controls. These results support the measurement implementation; they do not rescue the failed improvement hypothesis.

Interpretation

Why the original claim was retired

What remains valid

The live Moshi-to-GaussianTalker bridge, training-matched Mimi latent path, controlled ablation infrastructure, geometric audit, and renderer-identifiability checks remain substantive systems and measurement work.

What failed

Measured geometric non-similarity did not translate into the registered residual association required to justify an improvement claim.

What was not run

The causal H4 renderer swap, the full supervision-transfer program, and several later research plans remained unexecuted or inconclusive.

Decision

Freeze the covariance-transport improvement claim and preserve the work as an archived negative-result study rather than describing it as ongoing positive 3DGS research.
