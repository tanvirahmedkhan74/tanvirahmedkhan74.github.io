Independent research

ERL — Evidence-Aware Retrieval and Memory

Can a memory item be useful because it routes retrieval, because it contains answer evidence, or because it does both?

2026–presentMulti-hop question answeringACL-targeted manuscript in preparation

Current status: active research. The manuscript is not accepted, and the central learned-routing result is negative under the registered protocol.

Research question

Can paired interventions isolate routing utility from direct evidence utility without changing the inspection budget or silently granting the system more access?

The project began with Emergent Retrieval Landmarks: the hypothesis that intermediate memories could acquire persistent value because traversing them helped recover other evidence. A prospectively fixed comparison on LoCoMo tested learned transit routing against BM25 under the same inspection budget. The methods tied at full-support recall, and learned transit was worse at the tighter budget. The line stopped at its registered gate.

The subsequent work is therefore a controlled failure analysis, not a successful-routing claim. It decomposes evidence access into reachability, discovery, selection, and reading; tests repairs; and retains later negative results when stronger baselines erase the apparent increment.

Intervention

Separating route access from context access

CRV/DEV is a paired measurement instrument, validated synthetically. It is not evidence that learned routing works on real data.

Route-off

Blocks an intermediate memory's outgoing expansion while preserving its eligibility to appear in the reader context.

Evidence-off

Preserves traversal through the memory while removing its eligibility to contribute direct reader evidence.

Matched budget

Retrieval arms share an explicit ledger of inspected nodes, score operations, hops, and context size.

Paired estimation

Per-query deltas are summarized with BCa confidence intervals and bank-aware statistical checks.

Not established

The study does not show that ERL routing improves real-data retrieval, that selection is universally the dominant bottleneck, that the selector transfers zero-shot, or that larger readers causally benefit more. End-to-end E11 and mechanism-audit E12 results do not yet exist.

Current status

What remains unresolved

The active manuscript treats the project as a controlled multi-hop QA failure analysis. Registered follow-ups include a shortcut/mechanism audit of the historical 2Wiki result and a same-dataset end-to-end chain; neither is currently reported as complete. A separate prospective direction is to apply CRV/DEV to an existing graph-RAG system, where routing value could be tested on real data rather than only in synthetic validation.

No public paper link is provided because the manuscript remains an anonymous draft and the target cycle is unresolved.
